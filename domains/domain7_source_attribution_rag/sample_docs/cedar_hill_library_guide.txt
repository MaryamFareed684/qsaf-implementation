Cedar Hill Community Library - Visitor Guide

Members may borrow up to 6 items at a time for a period of 14 days. Each item can be renewed once online unless another member has reserved it. Overdue items cost 20 cents per day, up to a maximum of 5 dollars per item. Membership is free for residents of Cedar Hill. Visitors from other towns pay an annual fee of 12 dollars. A membership card must be shown to borrow any item. The library is open from 9 am to 7 pm on Tuesday to Friday and from 10 am to 2 pm on Saturday. It is closed on Sunday and Monday. Computers can be booked for up to 1 hour per day. Printing costs 10 cents per page.
